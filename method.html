Method

How a tool earns a score

Published before the first review, so the method cannot be bent to fit a verdict.

Before we write

  • We buy the plan a small team would buy, with our own money, and use it for real work for at least two weeks.
  • Every review states the plan, the price paid, the invoice month and the version tested.
  • Vendors do not see a review before it is published and cannot pay for placement.

The score, out of 100

PartPointsWhat we look at
Does the job35The same fixed tasks for every tool in a category, with the outputs shown.
Real cost20Yearly cost for 1, 5 and 15 seats, including minimums, caps and overage.
Data and privacy25The twelve points in the vendor check. "Not stated" scores zero for that point.
Leaving10Export formats, cancellation, reducing seats.
Running it10Setup time, admin controls, support response to one real question.

After we publish

  • Each review carries a "last checked" date. Prices and privacy terms are re-checked at least every six months.
  • Corrections are listed at the bottom of the page with a date. We do not edit silently.
  • If a vendor tells us something is wrong and shows evidence, we fix it and note it.

What a score is not

It is one small team's experience on one plan. It is not a security audit and not legal advice.
