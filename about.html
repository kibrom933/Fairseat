About Fairseat

Fairseat is an independent site run from Denmark. We review AI tools for freelancers and small teams, with the questions European buyers actually ask: real price, data location, retention and training.

How we make money

When we start reviewing, some links will be affiliate links: if you buy, the vendor may pay us a commission at no extra cost to you. Every such link will be labelled "Affiliate link (ad)". Commissions never change a verdict, and we never claim to have tested something we haven't.
