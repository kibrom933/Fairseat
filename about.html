About

Who is behind Fairseat

Fairseat is an independent site run from Denmark. It exists because buying AI tools for a small business in Europe means answering questions most review sites skip: what it really costs per year, and what happens to the data.

How we make money

Today: we do not. There are no ads and no affiliate links on the site.

When reviews are published, some links to vendors will be affiliate links. If you buy through one, the vendor may pay us a commission, at no extra cost to you. Each such link will be marked Ad: affiliate link right next to it. A tool can get a poor score and still have an affiliate link; a tool with no affiliate programme can get the top score.

Privacy

This site sets no cookies and loads nothing from third parties: no analytics, no fonts from other servers, no embeds. The calculator and the vendor check run in your browser. The calculator remembers your rows in your browser's local storage so they are there next time; use Clear to remove them.

Corrections

If something here is wrong or out of date, we want to know. A contact address will be added with the first review.
