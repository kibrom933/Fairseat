Tool 02

Vendor check

Twelve things to find out before client or staff data goes into an AI tool. Answer from the vendor's own pages: pricing, privacy policy, DPA, trust centre. Nothing you enter leaves this page.

1. Where is the data processed and stored?

Look for a named region (for example EU, Germany, US). 'Global infrastructure' is not an answer.

2. Is your data used to train models?

Check the default, not the opt-out. Check whether it differs between free, team and enterprise plans.

3. How long is data kept?

A number of days for content, and a separate answer for logs and backups.

4. Is there a data processing agreement?

For GDPR you need a DPA (Art. 28) if the tool processes personal data for you. Self-serve signing is a good sign.

5. Are sub-processors listed?

A public list with names, purposes and locations, including which AI model providers are used.

6. How are transfers outside the EU covered?

Typically the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses. It should be named.

7. Can you delete your data, and is it confirmed?

Self-serve deletion of single items and of the whole account, with a stated time until backups are purged.

8. Can you export your data?

A standard format (CSV, JSON, Markdown, audio files) without contacting sales.

9. Is there independent security evidence?

SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001 report, available at least under NDA. A badge without a report is a claim.

10. Can you control who has access?

SSO or at least enforced two-factor, roles, and a way to remove a leaver in one place.

11. Is the full price public?

Per-seat price, minimum seats, and any usage caps or credits stated on the pricing page.

12. Can you leave without penalty?

Monthly cancellation or a clear annual term; seats can be reduced; no auto-upgrade.
