What we will not do

  • Review a tool we have not paid for and used. There are no reviews on this site yet for exactly that reason. When the first ones come, they will show the invoice date and the plan we tested.
  • Fill a gap with a guess. When a vendor does not say where data is processed or how long it is kept, the review says not stated.
  • Hide the commission. Links that pay us will be labelled as ads, next to the link, every time. Details.
  • Rank by payout. The scoring method is published before the first score is. Read it.
