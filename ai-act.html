The EU AI Act for small teams

General information, not legal advice. Deadlines for some obligations have been under revision; check the official timeline before relying on a date.

Are you a "deployer"?

If your business uses an AI tool at work (a meeting notetaker, a writing assistant, a voice generator), the Act generally treats you as a deployer. Most everyday tools are not "high-risk", so your duties are light, but not zero.

What already applies

  • AI literacy: make sure the people using AI tools for your business understand them well enough to use them sensibly.
  • Banned practices: some uses are prohibited outright, such as emotion recognition in the workplace in most cases.

Transparency

If you publish AI-generated audio, video or images that look real, or let customers chat with an AI, people should be told. Plan for clear labels.

GDPR still matters most

For most small teams, the bigger day-to-day question is GDPR: where the vendor processes data, how long it keeps it, whether your data trains its models, and whether a data processing agreement is available. Every Fairseat review answers those four questions.
