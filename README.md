# Fairseat

Static site: the real yearly cost, idle seats and privacy terms of AI tools, for small teams in Europe.

- `python3 build.py` rebuilds every page from the bodies in `build.py`. No dependencies.
- Published from the repo root (`netlify.toml`). No cookies, no third-party requests; fonts are in `fonts/`.
- Rule: no review is published before the tool has been paid for and used. See `method.html`.
- The AI Act guide carries a "checked" date. Re-check the sources at the bottom before changing a date.
