Tool 01

Seat receipt

Add the AI tools you pay for. You get the yearly total, the cost of idle seats, and the saving from annual billing. Your numbers stay in this browser.

Monthly price is the per-seat price on monthly billing. Annual-plan price is the per-seat, per-month price when billed yearly; leave it empty if you do not know it. "Used" is how many seats were actually used in the last 30 days: most admin consoles show it.

Reading the receipt

Idle seats are licences bought for someone who tried the tool twice and stopped. If the idle line is more than a fifth of the total, drop seats before you negotiate price.

Annual billing usually lowers the per-seat price, but it removes your ability to drop seats mid-year. Take the saving only for tools where the used-seat count has been stable for three months.

What the receipt cannot see: usage-based charges (credits, tokens, minutes), VAT, and price changes at renewal. Check the invoice, not the pricing page.
